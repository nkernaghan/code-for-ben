# Market Researcher

Investigate market trends, competitive landscapes, sizing opportunities, and signals for emerging markets. This skill covers landscape mapping, trend analysis, on-chain metrics, sentiment tracking, and sizing estimation.

## Research Framework

### 1. Market Definition
- **Market:** What is it? (e.g., "Solana trading bot ecosystem")
- **Addressable market:** How big? (total TAM)
- **Serviceable market:** Our slice (SAM)
- **Serviceable obtainable:** Realistic capture (SOM)

### 2. Landscape Mapping
- **Competitors:** Who are they? (direct + indirect)
- **Market share:** Who has what % of revenue/users?
- **Positioning:** What makes each different?
- **White space:** What's not being served?

### 3. Trend Analysis
- **Growth trajectory:** Markets growing, flat, or declining?
- **Inflection points:** When did growth accelerate?
- **Cycles:** Boom/bust patterns? (especially crypto)
- **Signals:** What triggers market changes?

### 4. Sizing & Estimation
- **Bottom-up:** Count users/transactions, estimate value
- **Top-down:** Start from total market, estimate your piece
- **Comparable:** Similar markets, apply to this one

## Crypto Market Research

### On-Chain Metrics
- **Txn volume:** Total transaction count & USD value (Solana, Ethereum)
- **Active wallets:** Unique addresses transacting daily/weekly/monthly
- **TVL (Total Value Locked):** $ locked in protocols (DeFi)
- **Token flows:** Movement between wallets/exchanges (sentiment)
- **DEX volume:** Decentralized exchange trading volume

**Tools:** Nansen, Glassnode, Dune Analytics, CryptoQuant

### Market Cycles
- **Bull:** Prices rising, transaction volume up, new users
- **Bear:** Prices falling, volume declining, user exodus
- **Recovery:** Prices consolidating, builders building (strong signal)
- **Mania:** Retail FOMO, alts exploding, scams proliferating

### Regulatory & Macro Signals
- **SEC actions:** Enforcement against protocols or tokens
- **Central bank policy:** Interest rates, inflation (affects risk appetite)
- **Geopolitical:** China crackdowns, EU regulations, US policy changes
- **Mainstream adoption:** Banks offering crypto, corporations buying Bitcoin

### Market Share Estimation

**Solana ecosystem:**
- Token price × circulating supply = market cap
- DeFi TVL on Solana ÷ total DeFi TVL = market share
- Solana txn volume ÷ total blockchain volume = adoption

## Sports Betting Market Research

### Market Sizing
- **Legal markets:** US states with sports betting (40+)
- **Total handle:** $$ bet (not revenue)
- **Revenue:** House edge × handle (typically 5-7%)
- **Growth:** CAGR year-over-year

### Competitive Landscape
- **Sportsbooks:** DraftKings, FanDuel, BetMGM, Caesars, others
- **Market share:** By state and sport
- **Odds models:** Who has the sharpest lines?
- **Predictive tools:** Model providers (Covers, TheSportsGeek, etc.)

### Modeling Accuracy Benchmarks
- **Public models:** Vegas lines (55-60% accuracy on spreads)
- **Sharp models:** 52-55% accuracy (beating the line)
- **ML models:** 55-65% accuracy (better than average)
- **Ensemble:** Multiple models combined (60-68% possible)

### Signal Detection
- **Line movement:** Sharp money (early) vs. public (late)
- **Public bias:** Favorites often over-bet (fade public)
- **Value opportunities:** Your 60% vs. Vegas 55% = +EV bet
- **Tournament advantage:** Bracket beating requires 53%+ accuracy

## Sentiment Analysis

### Measurement Methods
- **Social media:** Twitter/X sentiment (bullish/bearish mentions)
- **News sentiment:** Natural language processing on headlines
- **On-chain behavior:** Whale wallets buying/selling
- **Options market:** IV, put/call ratio (fear gauge)

### Tools
- **Crypto:** Santiment, LunarCrush, Glassnode (on-chain)
- **Social:** Twitter API, Brandwatch (mention volume)
- **Market:** FEAR index, VIX proxy (implied volatility)

### Caution
- **Sentiment lags reality:** Memes don't drive market (prices do)
- **Survivorship bias:** Only successful bets get hyped
- **Correlation ≠ causation:** Sentiment can follow price, not lead it

## Competitive Intelligence

### Sources
- **Company websites:** Product features, pricing, positioning
- **Social media:** Community size, engagement, sentiment
- **Customer reviews:** What users like/dislike
- **Job postings:** Hiring for new products (early signal)
- **Press releases:** Partnerships, funding, pivots

### Competitor Analysis Checklist
- [ ] Business model (subscription, fees, freemium?)
- [ ] Target user (retail, professional, enterprise?)
- [ ] Core features (what do they focus on?)
- [ ] Pricing (how much do they charge?)
- [ ] Community size (users, followers, engagement)
- [ ] Recent changes (product launches, pivots)
- [ ] Strengths (what do they do well?)
- [ ] Weaknesses (what's missing?)
- [ ] Threats to their business (technical, competitive, regulatory)
- [ ] Opportunities for differentiation

## Market Trend Detection

### Leading Indicators
- **Developer activity:** Github commits, discussions (builders building)
- **Token unlocks:** Large holder selling triggers (vs. accumulating)
- **Institutional adoption:** Large buy signals (Microstrategy, corporate treasuries)
- **Infrastructure improvements:** Network upgrades, fee reductions

### Lagging Indicators
- **Price action:** After trend establishes (high risk, low reward)
- **News coverage:** Mainstream media adoption (trend already priced in)
- **Retail FOMO:** Latest to the party (peak mania)

## Common Research Mistakes

❌ **Confirmation bias:** Only seeking data that supports your thesis
❌ **Recency bias:** Over-weighting recent trends (bull market ≠ forever)
❌ **Survivorship bias:** Studying winners, ignoring failures
❌ **Outdated data:** Using last year's numbers for fast-moving markets
❌ **Single source:** Relying on one provider for truth
❌ **Ignoring externalities:** Macro, regulatory, geopolitical changes
❌ **No second opinion:** Peer review catches blind spots
